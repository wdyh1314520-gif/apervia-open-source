# Community Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to providing every participant with a friendly, professional, and evidence-based collaborative environment, regardless of differences in experience, identity, background, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

- Focus on the issue and evidence, and explain impact and tradeoffs clearly.
- Respect different experience levels and provide actionable feedback.
- Accept reasonable challenges and correct mistakes promptly.
- Protect user data, credentials, and undisclosed vulnerabilities.
- Remain constructive during disagreements; do not engage in personal attacks, harassment, or disparagement.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Threats, harassment, discrimination, insults, or persistent provocation.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Publicly disclosing unpatched vulnerabilities, real credentials, or user data.
- Spam, malicious disruption, or intentional attempts to mislead maintainers or users.
- Other conduct clearly inappropriate for a professional collaborative environment.

## Enforcement

General community concerns may be reported privately to the repository maintainers through GitHub. Report security concerns according to the [Security Policy](SECURITY.md). Depending on the circumstances, maintainers may edit or remove content, warn participants, restrict interactions, or prohibit participation, while making reasonable efforts to protect reporter privacy.
